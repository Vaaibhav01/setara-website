About Us

SETARA Foundation is a research-driven action organization working at the intersection of environment, livelihoods, climate resilience, tribal development and sustainable enterprise.

Vision

A future where people and nature flourish together.

Mission

To catalyse environmental and social transformation through community-led action, research, innovation and sustainable enterprise.

Programs

Ecological Restoration

Forest, biodiversity and landscape restoration.

Climate Action

Adaptation, resilience and community climate solutions.

Livelihoods

Mahua, Tamarind, Sal Seed, Jackfruit and value chains.

Cooperatives

Institution building and producer enterprises.

Research

Evidence-based development and policy engagement.

Youth Leadership

Green entrepreneurship and skills.
